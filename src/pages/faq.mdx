# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Onam Cloud?

Onam Cloud is a sovereign cloud infrastructure platform. It is designed to give organizations choice over where infrastructure runs, where data resides, and how workloads are operated.

## What are OnPublic, OnPrivate, and OnBYOPC?

- **OnPublic** is a shared sovereign public cloud for cloud-native workloads and development environments.
- **OnPrivate** is a dedicated, single-tenant private cloud operated for an organization.
- **OnBYOPC** brings the Onam Cloud platform to customer-owned infrastructure in the customer's data centre.

See [Choose a deployment model](/docs/platform/choose-deployment-model) for a comparison.

## Where is the infrastructure located?

The Onam Cloud website lists the IN-WEST-1 region in Mumbai, India. Available regions and service options may change; confirm current availability with your Onam Cloud representative.

## Which workloads can the platform run?

Onam Cloud describes support for virtual machines, containers, and Kubernetes, with automation options that include APIs, Terraform, Ansible, and GitOps. Contact the team to confirm the capabilities available for your chosen deployment.

## How do I get account or implementation help?

For access, onboarding, architecture, or environment-specific guidance, [contact the Onam Cloud team](https://onamcloud.com/contact).
